# Project conventions

## Naming

A tier list category is named for **what is being ranked, and nothing
else**: "NFL Teams", "NFL Quarterbacks". Never a "Tier List" suffix.
Everywhere one of these titles appears, something beside it already says
what kind of thing it is - the rail above the board, the eyebrow on the
share card, the page it is a card on - so the suffix was the same two
words repeated on every line of every screen. This applies to both
`title` and `defaultListTitle` on a template; see `src/data/tierTemplates.ts`.
